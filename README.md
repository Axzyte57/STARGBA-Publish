# STARGBA-Publish
Mini Display Controller for Android and PC devices.

This is a Tool for reusing an Old Tablet/Phone as a screen Inside you PC CASE!!

this tool can show temperatures and other telemetry such as fan speeds,power draw and Many more. It can also show Gifs, Videos and controll the Device inside the PC

its also incredibly dynamic, supporting virtually infinite devices Over Bluetooth, WIFI, ADBbridge,and even USB

Its very customizable, SAY...YOU have a friend and about 10 more of them in the same room with Their PCs! and you Thought!!! Hey, lets see how many KILOWATTS our computers are using combined.. you can setup a PC as a master that is receaving All the DATA from the other Pcs and Displays them in a Single Device!

its very customizable to the point that i can show my Friends temps and he can Show Mine in Our PCs integrated Phone/Tablet Display

The main Qualitys of this programm is that its Low power and has a trully seperated Back and Front-end comunicating Only via Named pipes, its efficient and Very Easy to scale up!

This is version 0.04 of the program since the Android up was Just builded and hasnt even been debuged yet/nor polished!

the Tool will also support Custom Models in the form of a 32x32 or 64x64 PNGs that You assigh for a customized experience
